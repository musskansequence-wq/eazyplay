About Jumping Kingdom

Jumping Kingdom on the 5th floor of Galaxy Blue Sapphire Plaza, Greater Noida West, has a trampoline park with foam pits, a spider tower and wall climbing, a donut slide with an air bag, a wipe-out challenge and projector ball games. Toddlers aged 1–5 get their own soft-play area, and there's a café on site.

Highlights

  • Wipe-out challenge, basketball, projector games
  • Soft play just for ages 1–5
  • Café and birthday parties
  • Socks required; one adult per child
