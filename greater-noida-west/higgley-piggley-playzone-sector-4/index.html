About Higgley Piggley PlayZone

Higgley Piggley on the 5th floor of Galaxy Diamond Plaza, Gaur City 1 (Greater Noida West) is a play zone for ages 1.5–10 with 12 ft slides, trampolines, a zip line, a foam pit, a ball pool, a pebble pit, a sand pit, a tunnel maze, a Lego wall, an obstacle course and pretend play. A vegetarian café serves parents and kids.
