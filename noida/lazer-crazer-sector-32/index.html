About Lazer Crazer

Lazer Crazer on the 3rd floor of Wave City Centre, Sector 32, is built around a neon-lit laser tag arena, with VR rides (roller coaster, cricket, Beat Saber), PS5 gaming and an arcade zone alongside. It's best for kids 5 and up and works well for birthday groups, with a café for snacks.
