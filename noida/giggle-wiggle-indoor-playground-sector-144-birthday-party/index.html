About Giggle Wiggle Indoor Playground

Giggle Wiggle in Sector 144 (near Oxygen Business Centre) is a colourful play area for ages 2–9, with donut and tube slides, a zip line, a trampoline, a climbing wall, themed ball pits, a sand pit, a Lego corner and projector ball games. Parents can sit in the café, and there's a themed party room.
