About Play Hoppers

Hoppers is a play area near Step By Step School in Rohillapur, Sector 132, with slides, a ball pool, trampolines, an obstacle course and a zip line for kids from age 1. Parents can wait in the attached café, and there's a room for birthday parties. Closed on Mondays.
