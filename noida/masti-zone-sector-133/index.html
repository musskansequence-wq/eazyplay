About Masti Zone

Masti Zone at Paras One33, Sector 133, is a big games centre with bowling, laser tag, bumper cars, VR rides, shooting and archery games, trampolines and an arcade, plus a soft-play area for younger kids and a toy store. It's open till 10 PM, with a café and birthday party areas.
