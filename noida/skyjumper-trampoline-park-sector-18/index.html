About SkyJumper Trampoline Park

SkyJumper at Wave Mall, Sector 18, has over 30 connected trampolines across 4,000 sq ft, a foam pit, a basketball dunk zone, climbing structures, inflatable slides and a ball pool, with a separate play area for younger children. There's no upper age limit, so parents can jump too.
