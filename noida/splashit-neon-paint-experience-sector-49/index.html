About Splashit Neon Paint Experience

Splashit in Sector 49 (3rd floor, near Zudio and the Hanuman Murti) is a messy-art studio: splash glow paint in the black-lit Neon Splatter Room, paint in the Acrylic Room, decorate a teddy, or make a rug with a tufting gun. Protective suits are provided, the paints are non-toxic, and parents can join in.

Highlights

  • Neon Splatter Room with glow paints
  • Acrylic painting room
  • Teddy painting
  • Rug tufting (older kids and adults)
  • Protective suits provided; non-toxic paint
  • On-site parking
  • Ages 3 and up
