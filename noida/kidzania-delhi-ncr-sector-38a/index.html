About KidZania Delhi NCR

KidZania is a 97,000 sq ft kid-sized city at The Great India Place (Gate 11), Sector 38A, where children aged 2–16 role-play more than 90 real jobs: pilot at the aviation academy, doctor in the hospital, firefighter, police officer, radio jockey, banker and more. Kids earn and spend their own currency, KidZos, as they go. There's a food court inside, outside food isn't allowed, and it's closed on Mondays.
