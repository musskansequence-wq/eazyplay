About Tingaland

Tingaland is a big indoor play area on the 3rd floor of The Starling Retail in Sector 104, with a separate toddler zone for ages 1–4 and an activity zone of 20+ things to do, from jungle gyms and climbing walls to ball pits and slides. Older kids can add a session on the climbing wall, and the Tinga Café keeps parents comfortable while they wait.

Highlights

  • Tinga Toddler's Zone for ages 1–4
  • 20+ activities: jungle gym, ball pits, slides, climbing walls
  • Wall climbing as an add-on
  • Tinga Café for parents and kids
  • Themed birthday parties
  • Ages 1–10; one adult per child inside
