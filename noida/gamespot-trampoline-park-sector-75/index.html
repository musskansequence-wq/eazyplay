About GameSpot Trampoline Park

Gamespot on the ground floor of Spectrum Mall, Sector 75, is an action park for kids 6 and up: trampolines, a 3-storey climbing wall, a zip line, a giant slide and obstacle courses, plus VR, arcade games and air hockey. It stays open till 10 PM and has a café and a party room.
