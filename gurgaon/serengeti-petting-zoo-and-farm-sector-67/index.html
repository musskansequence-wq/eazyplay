About Serengeti Petting Zoo and Farm

Serengeti on Dhumaspur Road near Pinewood Studio (Badshahpur, Sector 67) is a petting farm where kids feed and pet rabbits, guinea pigs, goats, lambs, pigs, ducks and geese, visit an aviary, play in a sandpit with construction toys, bounce on a trampoline and paint canvases. There's a pond-side restaurant. It's open on weekend evenings only.

Highlights

  • Pet and feed rabbits, goats, lambs, pigs and ducks
  • Aviary with hens, guinea fowl and budgies
  • Sandpit with construction toys
  • Trampoline and canvas painting
  • Pond-side restaurant
  • Ages 1 and up; Sat–Sun evenings only

Good to know

  • Where: Sector 67, Gurgaon
