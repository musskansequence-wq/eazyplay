About Zootopia

Zootopia – The Funyard, near Urban Akhara and the Duke Horse Riding Club in Sector 58, is an animal petting farm with goats, sheep, rabbits, guinea pigs, hedgehogs and cows, and a big bird collection from ducks and emus to cockatiels and pheasants. Visits include a 45-minute guided tour and a sand pit, and every child takes home a small plant.

Highlights

  • Goats, sheep, rabbits, guinea pigs, hedgehogs, cows
  • Ducks, geese, emus, parrots and pheasants
  • 45-minute guided tour
  • Animal feeding (add-on)
  • Sand pit play area
  • Café and parking
  • Under 1s free; no outside food or pets
