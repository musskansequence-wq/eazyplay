About Game On Board Cafe

Game On Board on the 1st floor of Ocus Quantum, Sector 51, is a board-game café with a library of 200+ games, from Catan and Ticket to Ride to Scrabble and Monopoly, played in themed rooms. Staff explain the rules, it suits kids 6 and up, and it hosts tournaments and birthday parties. Closed on Mondays.
