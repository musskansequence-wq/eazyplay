About iSkate by Roseate

iSkate by Roseate on the 6th floor of Ambience Mall, Gurgaon, is an indoor ice-skating rink. Sessions last about 45 minutes with skates provided, and coaches are around for first-timers. It suits kids from about 4 years up and is open daily from 10 AM till late evening.
