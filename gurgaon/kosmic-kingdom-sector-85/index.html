About Kosmic Kingdom

Kosmic Kingdom at Iris Broadway, Sector 85, has a soft-play area for ages 1–10 (slides, ball pits, ball showers) and a trampoline park with a foam pit for ages 7–14, plus a climbing wall, a LEGO wall, a sensory sand pit, 3D interactive games, basketball, a ball-shooting gallery and a pretend supermarket. The café serves pizza, juices and coffee.

Highlights

  • Soft play with slides and ball showers (ages 1–10)
  • Trampolines with foam pit (ages 7–14)
  • Climbing wall and LEGO wall
  • Sensory sand pit and 3D games
  • Basketball and ball-shooting gallery
  • Pretend supermarket
  • Café and private party rooms; socks required

Good to know

  • Groups: 25 kids + 30 adults
  • Where: IRIS Broadway, Sector 85, Gurgaon
