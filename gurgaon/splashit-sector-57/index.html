About Splashit

Splashit on the 1st floor of Block H, Sector 57 (opposite Good Luck Society), is a messy-art studio with a black-lit Neon Splatter Room, where kids throw, spin and hand-paint with glow paint, and an Acrylic Art Room where they paint a piece to take home. Suits and washbasins are provided; best for ages 5 and up.

Highlights

  • Neon Splatter Room with glow paint
  • Spin art, shooting art and hand art
  • Acrylic painting to take home
  • Protective suits and washbasins
  • Non-toxic paints
  • Ages 5 and up
